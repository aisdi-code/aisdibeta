⚠️ Safety FirstSECTION 02
🚨
CRITICAL — READ BEFORE TOUCHING ANYTHING Engine OFF. Ignition key removed. 🔩 Kill Switch in OFF position. Bike in NEUTRAL. 🔩 Wheel Chocks placed front & rear before lifting.
  • Place stand under the main frame cross-member just behind the engine oil pan — NOT under the oil pan itself.
  • Raise until both wheels have 1–2 inches of clearance. Rock the bike gently before working.
  • Keep a helper nearby or use chocks against the stand legs for extra stability.
FRONT WHEEL REMOVALSECTION 03
ℹ️
SETUP NOTE 2014 V-Star 950 Touring: single front disc, left side only. NO ABS. Speedo is cable-driven from left fork — no sensor on wheel hub.
F1

Loosen the Axle Nut — Before Lifting

With the front wheel on the ground, break the 🔩 Front Axle Nut loose counter-clockwise. Use a 🔩 Breaker Bar or impact — it's torqued to ~59 ft-lb from factory.

💡
PRO TIP Loosen it while the tire is still on the ground — the tire holds the axle still. Much easier than fighting it in the air.
F2

Remove the Front Brake Caliper

The 🔩 Front Brake Caliper is on the left fork lower. Two 12mm bolts hold it. Remove both and slide the caliper off the disc.

⚠️
WARNING Hang the caliper with a zip tie from the fork — never let it dangle by the 🔩 Brake Line. A kinked hose = no brakes.
F3

Fender Clearance Check

The 🔩 Front Fender does NOT need to be removed. There is enough clearance to angle the wheel forward and down past the fender. Have a helper support the wheel.

F4

Remove the Axle Nut & Axle

Remove the 🔩 Front Axle Nut and 🔩 Flat Washer completely. Then tap the 🔩 Front Axle out from the LEFT using a 🔩 Rubber Mallet and wooden dowel — slides left-to-right. Catch it.

Front Axle — Removal Direction
F5

⚡ Spacers — Do NOT Lose These

Two 🔩 Front Axle Spacers will fall free as the axle slides out. Photograph and label them LEFT / RIGHT before bagging.

🚨
CRITICAL Swapped or missing spacers cause the wheel to sit off-center, contacting the rotor or fender. BAG AND LABEL: LEFT SPACER / RIGHT SPACER.
ℹ️
NO ABS — No tone ring, no ABS sensor wire, no reluctor ring to worry about. Just spacers.
F6

Withdraw the Wheel

Lower the 🔩 Front Wheel forward and down past the fender. Set on a clean surface — never rest it on the brake disc face.

💡
PRO TIP Rest the wheel on a folded shop towel with the rotor facing UP. Rotor face-down on concrete = warp or scratch.
F7

Remove the Front Rotor

Six 10mm bolts hold the 🔩 Front Brake Rotor to the hub. Loosen in a star pattern. On reinstall: apply 🔩 Loctite 243 to threads, torque to 16–20 ft-lb.

Front Rotor — 6-Bolt Star Pattern
REAR WHEEL REMOVALSECTION 04
ℹ️
BELT DRIVE NOTE The V-Star 950 uses a toothed rubber 🔩 Drive Belt. No grease. No sharp bends. Route and tension must be preserved on reassembly.
R1

Loosen the Rear Axle Nut — Before Lifting

Loosen the 🔩 Rear Axle Nut (24mm, counter-clockwise) before the wheel is fully off the ground. Torqued to ~80 ft-lb — use a breaker bar.

💡
PRO TIP Put the bike in gear while it's still on the ground — rear wheel won't spin and you can break the nut loose solo.
R2

Remove the Rear Brake Caliper

The 🔩 Rear Brake Caliper is on the right side of the swingarm. Two 12mm bolts hold it. Remove both, slide caliper off disc, hang with a zip tie — never by the brake line.

R3

Photograph the Belt Adjuster Positions

Before touching the 🔩 Adjuster Blocks, photograph BOTH sides. Count the threads showing. They must return to the exact same position or your wheel will be crooked and the belt will wear unevenly.

R4

Back Off the Adjuster Blocks

Loosen the lock nuts and back the adjuster bolts out equally on both sides, pushing the axle forward toward the engine to create slack in the 🔩 Drive Belt.

⚠️
MATCH BOTH SIDES Back both adjusters out the same number of turns. Mismatched adjusters = crooked wheel = belt wears in weeks.
R5

Remove the Axle Nut & Axle

Remove the 🔩 Rear Axle Nut and flat washer completely from the right side. The 🔩 Rear Axle slides right to left — tap gently from the right with a rubber mallet. Support the wheel as you pull the axle clear.

R6

Slide Wheel Back — Remove the Belt

Push the wheel rearward to create slack, then slip the 🔩 Drive Belt off the 🔩 Rear Pulley. Let the belt hang inside the swingarm. Slide the wheel rearward and out.

💡
PRO TIP Clip the belt to the swingarm with a zip tie while you maneuver the wheel free — it will try to drop.
R7

⚡ Cush Drive Dampers — Do NOT Lose These

As the wheel comes off, the 🔩 Cush Drive Dampers (6 rubber blocks in the rear hub) will fall free. Collect ALL of them into a labeled bag immediately.

🚨
CRITICAL — 6 RUBBER DAMPERS If even one is missing on reassembly, you will get a loud clunking noise under acceleration and eventual hub damage. Count all 6. Bag them.
Cush Drive — Rear Hub (6 Dampers)

Also collect the two 🔩 Rear Axle Spacers — one per side. Bag and label LEFT / RIGHT.

R8

Remove the Rear Rotor

Six 10mm bolts hold the 🔩 Rear Brake Rotor to the hub. Star pattern. Clean mating surface with 🔩 Brake Cleaner. Reinstall with 🔩 Loctite 243, torque to 16–20 ft-lb.

✅ Reassembly RemindersSECTION 07
🚨
PUMP THE BRAKES BEFORE ROLLING After installing new pads and rotors, pump each brake lever/pedal until firm resistance. This seats the pads. If you roll without doing this, you have NO brakes.
1Clean rotor mounting faces with 🔩 Brake Cleaner. Dry completely before installation.
2Install rotors with 🔩 Loctite 243 on bolt threads, torque 18 ft-lb star pattern. Keep Loctite off rotor surface.
3Rear only: Reinstall all 6 🔩 Cush Drive Dampers in hub. COUNT THEM. Then reinstall the sprocket carrier.
4Apply 🔩 Anti-Seize to axle threads only. Keep it off the spacers and bearings.
5Reinstall LEFT spacer first, slip axle through, then RIGHT spacer, then seat axle through right side.
6Rear only: Re-route 🔩 Drive Belt over rear pulley before seating wheel. Confirm belt fully seated in pulley teeth — no skipped teeth.
7Rear only: Set 🔩 Adjuster Blocks to photographed positions on BOTH sides equally. Tighten lock nuts.
8Thread axle nut hand-tight, then torque: Front 59 ft-lb / Rear 80 ft-lb. Anti-seize on threads.
9Reinstall 🔩 Front Caliper and 🔩 Rear Caliper over rotors. Torque caliper bolts 22 ft-lb.
10Spin each wheel by hand — should rotate freely with slight pad drag. No grinding, binding, or wobble.
11PUMP BRAKES. Front lever until firm. Rear pedal until firm. Repeat. Do not skip this step.
12Belt tension: Correct deflection is 10–15mm at mid-span with light finger pressure. Adjust via adjuster blocks if needed, recheck torque.
13Oil change: drain old oil, install new 🔩 Crush Washer at 31 ft-lb, new 🔩 Oil Filter hand + ¾ turn, fill 3.2 qt with filter change.
14Lower off stand. Brake bedding: 5–6 moderate stops from ~30mph, let cool, then 5–6 from ~45mph. Brakes will feel dramatically better after bedding.
💡
BELT TENSION REMINDER Recheck belt tension after 200–300 miles — new tires can settle wheel position slightly. Correct: 10–15mm deflection at mid-span with light pressure.
